# XHS Research Playbook

Use this reference for live research tasks. Keep the source log strict: no body read, no evidence.

## Official Source Map

Treat these as primary official surfaces when publicly accessible:

- `www.xiaohongshu.com` - public Xiaohongshu web pages, official notes, announcements, policy pages, and app-facing content.
- `creator.xiaohongshu.com` - creator center / creator service platform, when accessible.
- `pgy.xiaohongshu.com` and `pro.xiaohongshu.com` - 蒲公英 / brand-creator collaboration surfaces, when accessible.
- `ad.xiaohongshu.com`, `ad-market.xiaohongshu.com`, and related official ad docs - 聚光 / commercial advertising / marketing API surfaces, when accessible.
- `ark.xiaohongshu.com` - 千帆 / merchant operations / ecommerce backend surfaces.
- `gaia.xiaohongshu.com`, `zhaoshang.xiaohongshu.com`, `brand.xiaohongshu.com`, and `merchant.xiaohongshu.com` - merchant onboarding, brand/shop entry, and merchant operation surfaces, when accessible.
- `redlive.xiaohongshu.com` and official live-operation links - live commerce / store livestream management surfaces, when accessible.
- `school.xiaohongshu.com` - 小红书大学 / merchant learning / open platform docs and courses.
- `miniapp.xiaohongshu.com` - 小程序开放平台 docs, rules, and governance policies.
- `security.xiaohongshu.com` - security response center and related official security policies, if relevant.
- Official-hosted PDFs/CDNs such as `*.xhscdn.com`, `*.xhscdn.net`, `finance-public.xiaohongshu.com`, `picasso-static.xiaohongshu.com`, and `fe.xiaohongshu.com`.

For CDN or file-hosted materials, mark them official only when the document itself, the hosting path, or an official linking page supports Xiaohongshu authorship. Otherwise label them "官方托管待核验" and avoid using them as sole authority.

## Cross-Platform Official Channels

Use these as additional discovery and evidence channels only after verifying official identity from the channel profile, platform verification badge, account subject, an official Xiaohongshu page linking to it, or consistent corporate contact/domain evidence.

- 小红书站内官方账号 / 官方薯矩阵: search inside `www.xiaohongshu.com` and, if available, the app/web UI for official accounts related to creator service, community rules, 蒲公英, 专业号, 聚光, 商家, 直播, 活动, and客服. Open the note/profile body and verify the official badge or account subject before citing.
- 微信公众号 / 视频号: search WeChat, Sogou Weixin, and web-indexed `mp.weixin.qq.com` pages for official business, creator, merchant, course, and rule content. Open the article body and verify the account identity; screenshots or reposts are not enough.
- 微博、B站、抖音、快手、知乎、LinkedIn, and event pages: use only when the account is verified as Xiaohongshu/小红书 or the page is linked from an official domain. These channels are useful for public courses, live classes, conference talks, case releases, and policy announcements that may not be indexed on Xiaohongshu domains.
- App stores and official download pages: use for product positioning, app descriptions, version-release clues, and official support contacts, not for detailed operations policy unless the text directly says so.
- Government/registry surfaces: use ICP, public-security filing, CAC algorithm filing, app-store compliance, and official site footer links to verify identity or recommendation-related registrations. Do not infer platform operating tactics from filings unless the filing text directly supports it.

## Source Priority

1. Platform rules, user/service agreements, governance rules, official announcements, and help-center policy pages.
2. Product/platform docs from creator center, 蒲公英, 千帆, 商家学习中心, 小红书大学, and 小程序开放平台.
3. Product docs from 聚光, 专业号, 商家入驻, 直播管理, 商业开放平台, and service-market/open-API surfaces.
4. Official course pages, live-class pages, training documents, instructor materials, and official PDFs.
5. Official Xiaohongshu account posts, app-published guides, WeChat articles, videos, or conference/event pages whose author/platform identity can be verified.
6. Non-official media, agencies, SEO pages, community posts, and reposted course notes. Use only as pointers or labeled auxiliary context.

## Fetching Rules

- Open every relevant official result, not just the top result.
- For a page with navigation, open clearly relevant child links such as "规则", "课程", "帮助", "公告", "新手", "创作者", "商家", "直播", "店播", "商品笔记", "搜索", and "流量".
- For PDFs, download or open the file and extract text from the PDF body. Record page number when available.
- For JS-heavy pages, try rendered browser inspection, mobile/desktop variants, and same-domain linked docs. If the body still cannot be read without login, mark it inaccessible.
- For WeChat articles, official account posts, or video pages, capture the account name, verification/subject evidence, article title, publish date, and URL. If account identity cannot be verified, cite it only as non-official or "官方身份待核验".
- For official account notes inside Xiaohongshu, verify the profile/account badge when possible; otherwise state that the account authorship could not be independently verified from the accessible page.
- Deduplicate by canonical URL and document title. Keep the newest duplicate unless older copies contain unique relevant claims.
- Do not use third-party pages that merely repeat official claims unless the original official page is unavailable; even then, label them non-official.
- For broad research, stop only after at least two discovery routes have been attempted for each relevant bucket: domain search, official navigation, cross-platform official account search, PDF/CDN search, or rendered-page exploration.

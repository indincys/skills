# Output Field Patterns

Use this reference to choose fields for common web-collection deliverables. Do not copy every field by default; include fields only when they improve understanding, comparison, reuse, verification, or decision-making.

## General Case Or Inspiration Library

- Item name
- Category or cluster
- Source link or discovery note
- What it is
- Why it is relevant
- Core mechanism or tactic
- Transferable insight
- Evidence strength
- Caveats or limits
- Tags

## Social Media Account Or Creator Case

- Account or creator
- Platform and region/language
- Niche and audience
- Content format
- Positioning hook
- Growth or engagement signal
- Monetization path, if visible
- Funnel or conversion mechanism
- Repeatable tactics
- Risks, saturation, or dependency
- Source links

## Viral Title, Hook, Or Content Pattern

- Pattern name
- Example title or hook
- Psychological driver
- Structure or formula
- Best-fit topics/audiences
- Why it spreads
- Adaptation examples
- Misuse risks
- Related patterns

## GPT Image Or Video Use Case

- Use case
- User/job-to-be-done
- Input needed
- Output format
- Workflow steps
- Quality criteria
- Tool/model fit
- Commercial or creative value
- Constraints, risks, or policy concerns
- Example prompt or production note, if useful

## Monetized Content Account

- Account or brand
- Niche
- Audience pain/desire
- Content engine
- Monetization model
- Product/offer
- Funnel path
- Proof signals
- Estimated defensibility
- Replication difficulty
- Opportunity angle

## SaaS Or Enterprise Service Case

- Company/product
- Customer segment
- Pain point
- Product promise
- Pricing or packaging signal
- Acquisition/GTM channel
- Business model
- Public traction signal
- Differentiation or moat
- Weakness or risk
- Opportunity lesson

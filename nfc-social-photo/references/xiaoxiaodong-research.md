## Extracted Prompt Craft

1. Start with a mechanism, not a noun. A strong prompt says what kind of visual relationship should exist: breathing field, occlusion window, material emergence, edge crop, close object plus empty space, or third-person observation.
2. Assign roles. The subject, background, texture, light, and small marks each have jobs. A strong prompt tells the model what the viewer reads first, second, and last.
3. Build from semantic material. Colors, props, and texture come from the subject's meaning, use case, material, or emotional tone. They are not random decoration.
4. Use edge behavior. Crop, partial cover, foreground blockage, or subject entering from an edge creates discovery, not showroom staging.
5. Add controlled imperfection. Grain, roughness, hand-arrangement, worn surfaces, uneven edges, natural shadows, and material thickness make the image less synthetic.
6. Make the image useful or shareable. Place, time, daily mood, a recognizable life moment, or a clever physical relation gives the viewer a reason to pause.
7. Long prompts work because they reduce ambiguity. They state composition, light, material, hierarchy, negative constraints, and adaptation rules.

## What To Transfer

- Use long-form prompt structure.
- Define a visual mechanism before shot details.
- Make the NFC card-sleeve charm interact with a real surface, strap, hand, card, cable, zipper, table, bag, locker, or seat.
- Use plausible photography defects: small dust, fabric lint, micro scratches, imperfect alignment, soft motion blur, mixed light, real shadows.
- Create a "someone just noticed this" feeling through viewpoint and timing.

## What Not To Transfer

- Do not import heavy posters, giant title typography, advertising key visuals, promotional beverage layouts, magazine covers, or graphic collage density.
- Do not let color systems, text systems, or geometric structures overpower the product.
- Do not transform the product into a symbolic object, 3D render, illustration, collage cutout, or redesigned accessory.
- Do not copy prompt bodies as templates. Rebuild the method for realistic lifestyle product photography.
